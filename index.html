You will have 100 minutes to complete the 60-question practice simulation.

02

03

04

05

Manage your time and avoid spending too long on a single question.

06

MAKE EVERY QUESTION COUNT

Tips for Success

Keep these practical strategies in mind throughout your preparation.

◎

Understand the Requirement

Choose the answer that best satisfies the scenario, not simply an answer that is technically possible.

⇄

Focus on Key Differences

Review Import versus DirectQuery, measures versus calculated columns, Merge versus Append, and common data-model relationship concepts.

ƒx

Practice DAX in Context

Understand filter context, row context, CALCULATE, FILTER, and time intelligence rather than relying only on memorization.

◷

Manage Your Time

Eliminate unsuitable options, keep moving, and revisit difficult questions when necessary.

⌑

Review Security and Deployment

Understand row-level security, workspace permissions, report sharing, refresh configuration, and deployment concepts.
